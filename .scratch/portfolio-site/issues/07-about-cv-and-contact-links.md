# 07: About page as CV, plus contact links

**What to build:** A recruiter can read the author's CV on the About page and optionally download a PDF. They can reach the author from GitHub, LinkedIn and email links that appear site-wide. The default homepage gets a short intro (who, what) pointing to Projects, Blog and About.

See the spec: Content model (About); Homepage.

**Blocked by:** 01 (Site skeleton that builds, with a check command)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The About page is structured as a CV: summary, experience, skills and education, with placeholder content for the author to fill in.
- [ ] An optional CV PDF link points to a site asset. The page works if the PDF is absent.
- [ ] GitHub, LinkedIn and email links appear site-wide (header or footer social links).
- [ ] The default homepage has a short intro and links to the three other sections. No custom homepage template is used.
- [ ] No phone number or home address appears anywhere. Once ticket 05 is done, its personal-data scan covers this page.
- [ ] Built-site check: the About page exists and the social links render.
